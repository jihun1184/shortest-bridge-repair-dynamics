# D7D2-A4.3 — Parametric Representative Template Extraction

> **Historical result superseded by A4.3B:** the cut-convention defect
> reported here was repaired. Current result: 53/53 finite template
> consistency, followed by A4-L1a and the proved A4-L1b formal geometry.

## Method

For all 53 classes shared by k=6,7,8, extracted a template signature per
representative: `(tail_line, patch_shape)`, where `tail_line = (y,z)` of
the satellite's straight thin-tail run, and `patch_shape` is the
translation-normalized voxel shape of whatever remains of the satellite's
current component beyond that thin tail (the "thick" part, if any).

Tried both **arbitrary first-found** representatives (from BFS order) and
**canonical minimum-size** representatives (fewest total points, tie-break
by sorted coordinates) per class, to rule out representative-choice as
the source of any inconsistency.

## Results

```text
Arbitrary (BFS-order) representatives:  28/53 classes k-consistent
Canonical (min-size) representatives:   29/53 classes k-consistent
Distinct (tail_line, patch_size) families found: 17 (both methods)
```

All families share `tail_line=(2,0)` (expected — the satellite always
sits at that line for this ray). Patch sizes cluster sensibly:
`{0: 22 classes, 3: 6, 2: 5+2, 4: 3, 6: 2+1+1, 7: 2+1+1, 10: 2+1, 13: 1+1,
14: 1, 16: 1}` — a manageable ~17 families, as hoped, when the template
*is* consistent.

## Honest gap

**Switching to canonical (min-size) representatives barely improved
consistency (28→29 out of 53).** This means the remaining ~24 classes'
inconsistency is very likely not a representative-choice artifact — the
`(tail_line, patch_shape)` signature itself is not yet the right
invariant, or the patch boundary (where the thin tail is defined to stop)
is not landing on a truly k-independent cut for those classes. This has
not been diagnosed further.

## What this does and doesn't establish

- Positive: for at least 28-29 of 53 classes, a clean parametric picture
  (`A_C ∪ corridor_C(k) ∪ B_C`, patch anchored, only corridor length
  varying) holds across all three tested k. These are good candidates for
  a first structural-case proof.
- Negative / open: roughly half the classes do not yet have a verified
  k-independent template under this signature. A4-L1 (the Parametric
  Representative Lemma) is **not** established for all 53 classes — only
  suggestively supported for about half of them.
- A4-L2 (uniform labeled-transition lemma) and A4-L3 (closure/
  exhaustiveness lemma) have not been attempted at all yet — per the
  roadmap they depend on A4-L1 being solid first.
