# C07-D7D1 — Partial Persistent-Absorption Closure Checkpoint

The full N2-reachable contraction closures were completed for 234 of the 262
N1-successor roots arising from the 131 depth-three states in the declared D7B

The remaining 28 roots, belonging to 14 D7B states, were interrupted because

The correct checkpoint status is **PARTIAL**, not `EXHAUSTED`.

## 2. Method

For each completed N1 successor \(Y\), the audit performed a state-deduplicated
breadth-first traversal of the full N2-only reachable closure. At every state
\(Z\), it regenerated all frozen atomic actions and asserted that:

1. every available action was N2-only, since \(Y\) and all N2 successors are
   Q2/Q3-valid and valid states have no N1 repair actions;
2. every successor was Q2/Q3-valid;
3. every action strictly reduced the number of current 6-components;
4. the absorption partition had exactly one nontrivial block;
5. \(\gamma_Z(\Delta)=\rho_Z(\Delta)-1\).

The five components of each root were assigned stable identities `0,...,4`.
Every reachable current component was then encoded by the subset of root
identities it contained. The result records:

- every current root-identity partition with multiplicity;
- every distinct absorption-block transition with multiplicity;
- component-count-indexed \(\rho\) profiles;
- \(R^*(Y)\);
- a SHA-256 digest of all exact state/action transition records.

No depth cap, action pruning, or compressed partition-only state quotient was
used. D7D0 guarantees termination; D7D1 enumerated the actual voxel-set closure.

## 4. Persistent-rank result within the completed subset

Exactly six completed D7B states have \(R^*=3\) for both of their N1
successors:

\[
\boxed{\{15,79,89,197,206,207\}.}
\]

Equivalently, the 12 persistent-rank-three roots are

```text
orbit 15:  repair 0, repair 1
orbit 79:  repair 0, repair 1
orbit 89:  repair 0, repair 1
orbit 197: repair 0, repair 1
orbit 206: repair 0, repair 1
orbit 207: repair 0, repair 1
```

For every one of these roots, the exact finite closure establishes

\[
\forall Z\in Reach_{N2}(Y),\quad
\forall\Delta\in A_{N2}(Z),\quad
\rho_Z(\Delta)\le3.
\]

This is the persistent quantifier requested in D7D1, but only for these 12
finite roots. It is not a growing-family theorem.

have fewer than five current components.

## 5. Relation to the depth lower bound

Every D7D1 root begins with five components. For the persistent-rank-three
roots, the conditional contraction bound gives

\[
d_{N2}(Y)\ge
\left\lceil\frac{5-1}{3-1}\right\rceil=2.
\]

This matches the known depth-three plans' residual structure: one initial N1
action followed by at least two N2 actions. It does not yield depth growth,
because the component count is fixed at five. A growing family with fixed
persistent rank is still required for D7D2.

## 7. Claim boundaries

The completed subset supports the following exact finite statements:

- each of the 234 listed roots has a fully enumerated N2 closure;
- 12 roots have persistent \(\rho\le3\);
- 222 roots have exact \(R^*=4\);
- all recorded partition/component identities and action-block transitions are
  available in the JSON checkpoint.

The following remain **OPEN**:

- the D7D1 classification of all 262 roots;
- a universal persistent absorption bound;
- a growing family with component count \(m\to\infty\) and fixed persistent
  rank;
- a universal finite constant atomic-depth bound;
- universal atomic completeness.

D7D2 was not started. D7C remains a theorem only for its declared 150-state
family, D6B3 remains non-exhausted, and D3E remains the valid full-certificate
counterexample.

## 8. Recommended continuation

Do not rerun the 234 completed roots. Resume only the 28 roots from the 14
listed orbit indices, preferably one orbit pair at a time with per-root output
files rather than four large roots simultaneously. This reduces peak memory
and makes each completion independently preservable. Only after those 28 roots
are complete should D7D1 be upgraded from `PARTIAL` to `EXHAUSTED`.
