  Six claim-oriented reproduction scripts plus generate_manifest.py.

reference_results/
  bridge_lemma_reference.json
    Archival bridge-lemma audit supplied with the original package. The
    runtime bridge check uses chain_L5_orbit_audit_reference.json instead.
  chain_L3_base_M0_reference.json
  chain_L4_base_M0_reference.json
  chain_L5_base_M0_reference.json
    Frozen baseline minimal-repair families. L3 and L5 were recovered
    verbatim from BUNDLE_91_OUT_FINAL; L4 was recovered from its
    cp10_base_recomputed record (whose legacy top-level key is "base").
  chain_L5_orbit_audit_reference.json
    Frozen 76-orbit audit recovered verbatim from BUNDLE_91_OUT_FINAL.
  disjointness_L3_L5_reference.json
    Generated by scripts/disjointness_L3_L5.py.
  disjointness_L4_reference.json
    Generated by scripts/disjointness_L4.py.
  section6_1_Fpair_M0_reference.json
    Generated by scripts/section6_1_split.py.
    These three files archive outputs freshly recomputed with this package.

Not distributed:
  .git/, __pycache__/, and outputs/. The scripts create outputs/ at runtime.
